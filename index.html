How to play

  • Survive 20 waves of angry potatoes in a giant kitchen. Beat the final boss to win. Then keep going in endless mode.
  • Your potato peeler shoots spinning peel blades. Reload before you run dry.
  • Kill quickly to build a combo (up to x5 points).
  • Hits and kills charge Peel Storm, a ring of giant peels that pierces everything.
  • After every wave pick one of three upgrades with the 1, 2 and 3 keys.
  • Grab butter to heal and Golden Spuds for double damage.
  • Every 5th wave a boss shows up. Watch the radar, stay moving, use cover.
